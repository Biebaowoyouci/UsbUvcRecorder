USB 摄像机录制 / USB Camera Recorder v1.7.3

中文 / Chinese

本版修复

1. 手机摄像头方向
- 修复 Camera2 SurfaceTexture 已带方向变换后，又重复加入 SENSOR_ORIENTATION=90°的问题。
- 横屏预览只抵消当前显示旋转，后摄和前摄方向均恢复正确。
- 修复前摄在 90°/270°方向下镜像轴错误的问题，保持屏幕左右镜像而不是上下翻转。

2. 手机摄像头全屏比例
- 单设备模式改为等比例铺满并居中裁边。
- 水平和垂直方向使用完全相同的缩放系数，不再拉长、压扁或缩成屏幕中间的窄条。
- 多设备分屏仍采用完整画面适配，保留画面外空白区域双击显示/隐藏控制按钮的交互。

3. 真机验证
- 设备：Xiaomi 25098PN5AC（pandora），USB ADB。
- 后置 Physical 4：4096×2160 30p，方向修复并等比例全屏显示。
- 前置 Camera 1：方向与镜像测试通过。
- 冷启动和 Camera2 会话无崩溃。

English

Fixes in this release

1. Phone camera orientation
- Fixed an extra SENSOR_ORIENTATION=90° transform being applied after Camera2's SurfaceTexture had already provided the sensor transform.
- Landscape preview now compensates only for the current display rotation, restoring the correct orientation for both rear and front cameras.
- Fixed the front-camera mirror axis at 90°/270° so the preview remains horizontally mirrored instead of flipping vertically.

2. Full-screen phone-camera aspect ratio
- Single-device mode now uses aspect-fill scaling with centered cropping.
- Horizontal and vertical scaling use exactly the same factor, preventing stretching, squashing, or a narrow image in the center of the screen.
- Multi-device mode continues to fit the complete image and preserves the empty-area double-tap gesture for showing or hiding controls.

3. On-device validation
- Device: Xiaomi 25098PN5AC (pandora), connected through USB ADB.
- Rear Physical Camera 4: 4096×2160 at 30p, with corrected orientation and proportional full-screen display.
- Front Camera 1: orientation and mirroring tests passed.
- Cold start and Camera2 sessions completed without crashes.
